NEURAL FORGE 2026

Official Intra-College AI & Autonomous Systems Hackathon Rulebook

Organized by Code Crafters Club • October 15–16, 2026

info 1. Event Overview & Eligibility

Neural Forge 2026 is the premier 2-day continuous AI build sprint organized by the Code Crafters Club, Department of Computer Science & Engineering, SRM TRP Engineering College, Tiruchirappalli.

  • Venue: 5th Floor Seminar Hall, SRM TRP Engineering College
  • Dates: October 15 & 16, 2026 (2-Day Continuous Sprint)
  • Team Size: 3 to 4 Members per team
  • Eligibility: Open to 1st, 2nd, and 3rd-year engineering students across all departments
  • Registration Fee: ₹150 per shortlisted team (payable after Round 0 idea clearance)

timer 2. Four Competition Rounds

ROUND 0: Idea Submission & Initial Screening (Pre-Hackathon)

Teams submit a structured 1–2 page PDF or Google Form proposal with problem statement, proposed architecture, target track, and intended NVIDIA models. Top submissions are shortlisted to participate in the on-site hackathon.

ROUND 1: Rapid Prototype Development (Day 1)

Teams build the core working MVP. Mentors review progress and architecture at designated checkpoints. At the end of Day 1 (by 9:00 PM), each team submits their progress milestone report.

ROUND 2: Surprise Engineering Challenge (Day 2 Morning)

A surprise technical requirement is unveiled simultaneously to all teams (e.g., multilingual voice interface, offline resilience, real-time safety guardrails, or telemetry integration). Teams have 1 hour to adapt their codebase.

ROUND 3: Final Live Demo & Technical Q&A

A 3-minute live demonstration before the jury panel followed by a 5-minute technical Q&A on architecture, model latency, error recovery, and engineering depth.

smart_toy 3. Mandatory NVIDIA AI + API Integration

Every team must integrate at least one NVIDIA AI model (such as Nemotron, Cosmos, GR00T, or Sonic) via the NVIDIA API into their live demo solution.

💡 Polyglot Freedom: Python is NOT mandatory. Teams are free to use JavaScript, TypeScript, React, Python, C++, Go, Flutter, or any stack of choice.

gavel 4. Official Judging Rubric (100% Total)

25%
Technical Execution

Code quality, model integration depth, and architectural resilience.

25%
Design & Usability

UI responsiveness, user workflow, and polish of the live interface.

25%
Potential Impact

Real-world relevance, scalability, and problem-solution fit.

25%
Quality of Idea

Creativity, novelty, and technical ambition of the project concept.

verified_user 5. Code of Conduct & Ethics

All participants must adhere to strict academic and technical integrity standards:

  • Original code developed during the hackathon hours. Pre-existing libraries and open-source models are permitted.
  • All repositories must include an open-source license (MIT, Apache 2.0, or MPL 2.0).
  • Plagiarism, fabricated demonstrations, or interference with other teams results in immediate disqualification.
  • Hardware projects must operate safely within seminar hall guidelines.
