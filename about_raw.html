Code Crafters Club

We are Code Crafters, a club where creativity, technology, and learning come together.

You don't need to be an expert in coding to join our club. Even if you don't know coding at all, that's completely okay! If you have the interest and curiosity to learn, just join us — we are ready to guide and help you grow.

We have already successfully conducted events like Symposium and Engineering Day, where we had the opportunity to learn, explore, and create together.

Currently, we are working on an exciting app inspired by the concept of Duolingo. In the upcoming days, we are planning to conduct a Hackathon and are also preparing for our App Launch.

"You don't have to be a coder to join Code Crafters. Just bring your interest — we'll help you build the skills!"
