Personal AI

This track focuses on building an always-on, private personal assistant that keeps the user's data under their own control. It should have persistent memory, reusable skills, access to tools and information the user chooses, and the ability to carry out tasks across daily workflows using NVIDIA open-source models, NemoClaw, and OpenShell.

Physical AI

EVERYTHING YOU NEED TO KNOW ABOUT NEURAL FORGE ’26

Verified answers about the Flagship Intra-College AI & Autonomous Systems Hackathon at SRM TRP Engineering College.

Neural Forge ’26 is a two-day intra-college AI engineering hackathon organized by the Code Crafters Club at SRM TRP Engineering College. Participants will identify real-world problems and develop innovative AI-powered solutions.

The hackathon is open to 1st, 2nd, and 3rd-year students of SRM TRP Engineering College. Each team must consist of 2 to 4 members.

Participants can choose from four tracks: Coding & Agentic Engineering, Best Apps & Agents, Personal AI, and Physical AI.

Every project must meaningfully use Nebius Token Factory or Nebius AI Cloud and at least one NVIDIA open-source AI model.

Yes. Teams must provide a working demo URL so judges can evaluate their project. Physical AI projects are exempt from the demo URL requirement.

Teams must submit a working AI project, project description, demo link, public source-code repository, and a public YouTube demo video of 3 minutes or less. The README must include setup, usage, technologies, and Nebius + NVIDIA integration. All required Devpost fields must be completed before the deadline.

Teams that are shortlisted for the hackathon will be required to pay a registration fee of ₹100 per team.

Projects will be evaluated based on Technological Implementation, Design & UX, Potential Impact, and Quality of Idea, with 25% weightage for each category.
